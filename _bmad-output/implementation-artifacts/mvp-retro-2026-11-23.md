# Retrospective: the MVP build (Epics 1–5, 8) and the 2026-11-22 playtest

There is no sprint-status file (status lives in GitHub, see CLAUDE.md), so this retro uses these sources:
- the 88 closed `Story ` issues, their label history, and their merged PRs (bodies, commits, comments);
- the 244 CI runs of 2026-11-21 and 2026-11-22;
- `_bmad-output/playtest-2026-11-22.md`;
- both implementation readiness reports and both sprint change proposals;
- the GDD decision log (#1–#53);
- the Story 3.11 tuning record (`3-11-tune-phases-1-3.md`).

This is the first retrospective, so there are no earlier action items to follow up.

**Outcome.** The code met every story's acceptance criteria, but the game failed its first real player, who
quit about 2 minutes into Colony. The plan worked as written: the sprint change proposal of 2026-11-23 calls
the wall "the design working as written, not a bug". So the lessons are mostly about the plan, the simulator
and the feedback loop, not about code quality.

## 2. What went well

- **The unattended loop works.** 88 stories went from issue to merged PR in strict issue order. No main breakage
  needed a revert, no check was weakened, and the run resumed cleanly across sessions, because all state lived
  in GitHub.
- **The readiness check before the build paid off.** The 2026-11-21 report fixed 2 critical and 3 major spec
  problems before any code. One was C1: the sim policy could not have reached Egg Laying or Combat. No story
  then had to be relabelled `ready-for-human`.
- **The decisions are on record.** Every PR has a *Decisions* section, and the content stories list what they
  added and which pillar it serves (decision #31). That is what made this retro possible.
- **TDD and invariant tests held.** PRs record failing-first runs. The mid-run `gds-test-review` (#372) found
  discipline problems (80 hard waits, 4 guarded assertions, balance pins, no touch coverage), not missing
  coverage. Fix PRs cleared them during the run.
- **The sim tooling (Stories 3.7–3.9) was built honestly.** It plays through real `actions/` and `resume()` with
  no shortcuts, and the reachability test checked that by deliberately breaking a guard (#399). Story 9.1 builds
  on it directly.

## 3. Q1: Why didn't the plan, Story 3.11 or `pnpm sim` catch the Phase 2 wall?

Five causes, each with its evidence.

1. **The simulated player wasn't the player who hit the wall.** `dailySchedule` (Story 3.7) is 6 check-ins of
   5 minutes a day across a 16-hour waking day, following the GDD's session model at the time (30–90 s
   check-ins, assumption 2). A real first session is 45–60 minutes of continuous play. The wall came about
   2 minutes into Colony, inside session 1. The sim player left and came back hours later, so the wall fell into
   a check-in gap and never showed as a wait. The report said exactly that: "Phase 2 = 21 min active spread over
   16 h real".
2. **The report counted the wall as a success.** Story 3.11 added `capacityCritical` with a target of **at least 1**
   (`pacingTargets.criticalMoments`) and printed "capacity-critical: 5 (target ≥ 1 each)". The only defect probe
   was "nothing affordable within 4 h". A capacity wall is not "nothing affordable", and a 5-hour wait across
   check-ins tripped nothing. Both verdicts said "ok".
3. **The tuning was one big jump, tested against that model only.** Story 3.11 changed `digMsPerRoom` from
   1,000 to 96,000,000 (×96,000, about 27 h per room per digger) and cut every yield from 0.25 to 0.01. That fixed a
   real problem: 13 diggers took the nest from 25 to 3,275 rooms in one check-in. But it turned digging from
   the main source of capacity into a trickle, leaving the Warehouse (1,500 wood + 1,000 stone at 0.01/s) as
   the only way past 25. PR #402 merged about 3 minutes after it opened, with no human read.
4. **The warnings sat in PR bodies.**
   - #400: "Phases 1–3 are far too fast".
   - #402, *Remaining gaps*: "Colony's real-time window is narrow … a player with fewer or shorter check-ins lands outside it".
   - #408: "The Colony window is narrow … small Phase 1 changes move it by one check-in either way".
   - #409: a purchase-order change "cascaded until seed 1's Combat ended on day 4 (SLOW)".

   The run's instructions say "don't ask questions, decide", and they gave no way to escalate a finding that
   wasn't a blocker. CI can't see a pacing report, so the only reader was a human who wasn't reading.
5. **No human played until all 88 stories were done.** The GDD put the playtest after the MVP. The 4 UI bugs
   fixed in #439 had been on screen for between 3 hours and a day of build time.

**Root cause:** the plan measured pacing with a model of a returning player, and its pass criteria rewarded
the bottleneck. Nothing outside CI fed back into the run. Epic 9 already fixes the model: `firstWeekSchedule`
and gate 1 ("no idle gap > 2 min before 30 min", Story 9.1, decision #44). This retro fixes the feedback loop
and the pass criteria (§6).

## 4. Q2: What slowed the unattended build down or made it go wrong?

**There are no "recurring review findings" because there was no review.** No story PR got a single review
comment. CI was the only gate, and CI checks behaviour, not what the player reads. The one review was the
mid-run `gds-test-review` (#372, issues #368–#371).

| Pattern | Evidence | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Playwright broke on specs the story didn't touch** | All 9 red runs were Playwright: 1.35 (×2, panel stagger and BUILD seeds), 2.3 (cold-load resume ran before UI listeners were bound), 3.4 and 3.5 (BUILD stock reads and log line), 4.8 (golden path missing the Midden), #386 (the 2.13 home-screen modal blocked every tap in the new mobile project), 8.4, 8.7 | A fix-up commit and a re-run each time. The run rule is to run only "the Playwright specs the change touches", so this breakage only showed up in CI. Every case was fixed at its cause |
| **Branches were built ahead of the queue** | The comment on #412: "prepped branches for 5.1, 5.2, 8.1, 8.2 and 8.8 are pushed". 8.2 "was built before 8.1 and Story 5.2". 8.8 merged before 8.6 | ADR renumbered in 1.13, 1.14, 2.12, 4.1 and 8.2; save step renumbered in 4.10 (v30); rebase conflicts; 8.4 and 8.7 re-pushed as `-v2` branches (CLAUDE.md forbids that since #431) |
| **Bugs the player could see passed every check** | The upgrade card printed `function GlyphFood…`. Story 4.6 put `RESOURCE_BY_ID.food.glyph` in a template string, and 8.2 later turned that glyph into a component. The DIG tooltip's "+1 capacity per second" was true when Story 1.23 wrote it and false after 3.11. The food readout's parts were rounded separately from its net (#434) | Found only by the playtest (#432–#434). TypeScript accepts any value in a template string, and invariant tests don't read rendered copy |
| **The spec and the code disagreed** | #390 ("the spec's tick example does not match the code"); #421 ("twenty" glyphs became 23 after Epic 4); readiness fixes C1/C2/M1–M3 | Handled inside each story and recorded in the PR. The readiness check caught the large ones before the build |
| **Balance pins in tests** | Test review: about 172 numeric `toBe(N)` in unit tests, including the fractional `antimatter: 0.1` (#283) | Cleaned by #387 and Story 3.3 |

**Speed.** Nothing slowed the build much: a story took about 17 minutes on average, CI about 3 minutes. The
cost of speed was depth of checking. Rules that would slow a story down (full e2e before every push, a stop
after tuning) were weighed against that and not adopted (§6).

## 5. The playtest (2026-11-22)

See `_bmad-output/playtest-2026-11-22.md`. Four bugs were fixed in #439 (#432–#435). The wall's root cause is in
§3. The decisions that followed (session shape, the DIG core, scale to thousands, sim gates) went through the
2026-11-23 correct course into Epic 9 (decisions #34–#50), and the resource-sheet decisions became #51–#52.
The playtest did its job; it came late.

| A3 | **Balance stories state their gates.** A story that changes balance names the `pnpm sim` gates it must keep and asks for before/after verdicts in its PR | CLAUDE.md, *Planning with BMAD* step 2 (applies when batch 2 is written) | Done in this PR |
| A4 | **Bounded critical moments.** A food- or capacity-critical target also has an upper bound on how long it may last | CLAUDE.md, same place | Done in this PR |
| A5 | **The sim gotcha.** `dailySchedule` check-ins model a returning player; judge session 1 with `firstWeekSchedule` and gate 1 | CLAUDE.md, *Testing gotchas* | Done in this PR |

**Considered and not adopted (owner's choice).** These stay here as options if the same problem comes back:
- Stopping the run for a playtest after a tuning story.
- Running the full Playwright suite before every push.
- A "no prepped branches" rule.
- A per-story check of what the player reads at 360 px.
- CLAUDE.md lines on "copy reads the balance" and "no JSX in template strings".

**Follow-ups** (listed here only; the owner files or promotes issues):

  non-primitive template-string values (cause of #432).
- F2: UI copy that states a rate, time or cost should read it from `data/balance/` (cause of #433). Audit the
  remaining tooltips once DIG lands.
- F3: the `N of 88 stories done` progress line in `run-all-stories.md` step 7 still says 88; Epic 9 extends the
