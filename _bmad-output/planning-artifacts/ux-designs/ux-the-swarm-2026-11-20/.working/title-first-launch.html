[ TAP TO WAKE THE QUEEN ]
v0.1 · press any key on desktop

First-launch title · Phase 1 amber

Plays once, on the very first launch. The boot lines type in, then T H E and the SWARM block letters appear, then a line of ants starts marching across the soil. Reload the page to replay it.

Tapping goes to black, then A QUEEN, ALONE., then the brood chamber, then the coach mark on LAY EGG.

Later launches skip the title and open on NEST, with the offline summary if you were away. Settings would have a "replay intro" option.

Everything fits the 36-column grid: the logo is 29 columns, so it stays legible at 360 px. With reduced motion on, all lines appear at once and the ants stand still.
