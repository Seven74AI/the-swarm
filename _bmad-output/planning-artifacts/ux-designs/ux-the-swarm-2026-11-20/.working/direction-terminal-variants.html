Hive Terminal: two variants at real phone size

Both use the terminal shell: monospace type, phosphor colors, 4-letter resource labels and [BRACKET] buttons. The difference is how the colony and the world are drawn.

1 · Softer hybrid: vector colony, terminal everywhere else

The nest, map and star map are drawn as simple glowing vector shapes in the phase color. The ants have real ant shapes, and big colonies become a dense glowing cloud rather than shaded blocks. Text, panels and buttons stay terminal. Map tiles are 40 px, close to the 44 px tap target, and tapping one opens a tile panel.

2 · Terminal + real map: ASCII colony, vector map and star map

The nest stays ASCII (and turns into density blocks for big colonies, as before). Only Frontier's Map and Stars switch to vector, because that's where tap targets and spatial layout matter. Same tile panel and star map in the same phosphor color.
