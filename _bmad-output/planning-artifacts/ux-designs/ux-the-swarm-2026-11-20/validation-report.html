THE SWARM — UX Design Validation Report

_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/ux-designs/ux-the-swarm-2026-11-20/DESIGN.md · _bmad-output/planning-artifacts/ux-designs/ux-the-swarm-2026-11-20/EXPERIENCE.md

Adequate. Usable as a contract once a short list of fixes lands; it does not yet source-extract cleanly (rubric walker). The shape is right, the voice and tone are consistent, every text-contrast claim checks out when recomputed, and the owner decisions (CONTINUE, instant allocation, ignore policy, first-launch cinematic, glyph + label) carry through both spines. What blocks clean extraction: the spines and frontmatter disagree on the sheet background; the NEST primary-action priority hangs on undefined terms ("critical", "critical path"); several components named in EXPERIENCE have no visual spec, and automation targets and research progress have no spec at all; Click Power has no home in Phase 1; and phase-invariant warn/highlight contradict identity rule 1. The rubric raised no critical findings.

The three extra reviewers move the picture from "adequate with fixes" to "needs one revision pass before hand-off", without calling for a redesign. Each adds one critical: accessibility finds a seizure-safety contradiction (hold-repeat inverts the full-width primary button at about 8 Hz); mobile legibility finds that the 36-column grid, 5-cell strip and vertical stack are tuned to exactly 360x640 at 100 % text, overflowing at 110 % text, at viewports under about 352 px, and in the real 510-560 px browser viewport, while the spec promises 200 % text with no reflow rule (accessibility independently flags the same reflow gap); code feasibility finds CONTINUE semantics undefined (latched or live, re-checked on tap, and Colony still has two exits in transitions.ts). Two reviewers independently show that scanlines and glow push dim text below AA, even though the token contrast table is accurate as written. Code feasibility also finds the check-in loop (Flow 2) unbuildable today: there is no resume-from-background path. Accessibility adds a missing focus model and screen-reader semantics for the terminal-text idiom. After de-duplication across all four reviews: 3 critical, 22 high, 41 medium, 26 low.

Accessibility review

review-accessibility.md

Verdict: not ready to hand off as-is. Needs one revision pass, no redesign. Baseline WCAG 2.2 AA, GAG, XAG. The base beats most idle games (no colour-only meaning, timers can be turned off, unavoidable events are untimed, sound off by default, reduced motion honoured, ×MAX for every hold-repeat). The gaps: a seizure-safety contradiction, no keyboard/focus/screen-reader semantics for the terminal idiom, blinking that never stops, single-key shortcuts, a hold-only confirm, and dim text so close to 4.5:1 that the spec's own scanlines push it under. Counts: 1 critical, 7 high, 12 medium, 8 low.

critical

C1. Hold-repeat makes the primary button flash 8+ times a second

EXPERIENCE → Game Feel & Juice; Interaction Primitives; Accessibility Floor
Holding BUY inverts a full-width 50 px button between bg and accent at ≥ 8 Hz: a 7.7–21:1 luminance swing, and in Combat a saturated red flash. WCAG 2.3.1. This contradicts the spec's own "nothing flashes faster than the 1 s cursor blink". A seizure-safety issue, not polish.
Fix: Invert once on press and hold until release; show repeats only through the value and throttled +N; add a DESIGN rule "no element changes luminance more than 3 times in any 1 s"; border-thicken under reduced motion; add a test counting class toggles during a 2 s hold.
high

H1. dim margin is too thin and scanlines push it below AA

DESIGN → colors.*-dim, Elevation & Depth
dim/raised is 4.61–4.69; with the scanline overlay the effective ratio is 4.22–4.31, about 3.4 on darkened rows, on 11–12 px text.
Fix: Retune dim to ≥ 5.3:1 on raised and ≥ 5.8:1 on bg, or keep scanlines off text panels; restate the claim with method; add a contrast unit test.
high

H2. Blinking never stops

DESIGN → Primary button, Do/Don't; EXPERIENCE → Flow 1 step 2, Reduced motion; mockups
The cursor blinks forever on every screen; neither reduced motion nor the CRT toggle covers it. WCAG 2.2.2.
Fix: Add cursor and prompt blink to both toggles (steady █), or stop blinking after 5 s of no input.
high

H3. No focus model

EXPERIENCE → Accessibility Floor, Desktop; DESIGN → Components
No focus style, order, sheet trap or return; pinned bars can obscure focus (2.4.11); decision sheets must never become a keyboard trap.
Fix: Add a Focus subsection and token: 2 px highlight outline, defined DOM order, role="dialog" sheets with trap and return, no Esc on decisions, scroll-padding-bottom.
high

H4. Single-key desktop shortcuts have no off switch

EXPERIENCE → Interaction Primitives → Desktop
Space and 1–5 fire on dictation and stray keys; Space also collides with activating the focused control. WCAG 2.1.4.
Fix: Add a shortcuts on/off and remap setting; Space fires the primary action only when focus is on body or the primary button.
high

H5. Hold-to-confirm has no non-hold alternative

EXPERIENCE → Interaction Primitives, Accessibility Floor; title-cinematic.html
An adjustable duration doesn't help voice or switch users; the cinematic skip and TAP TO WAKE are pointer-only divs.
Fix: Add "hold / two taps" confirm setting; Enter/Space hold fills the ring; make skip and wake real buttons; announce the ring.
high

H6. The terminal-text idiom will be read out literally

DESIGN → Meter, Primary button, Event line, Shapes, Stepper
Screen readers will read "left bracket full block … middle dot", "greater than buy warehouse", and runs of box drawing; typing effects announce letter by letter.
Fix: aria-hidden all decorative characters; meters as progressbar with aria-valuetext; labelled stepper buttons; set full strings at once in the accessible node; real table/list markup.
high

H7. The 36-column grid conflicts with 200 % text and 320 px reflow

DESIGN → Typography, Layout & Spacing; EXPERIENCE → Accessibility Floor
At 200 % only about 19 columns fit a 360 px viewport; reflow is tested at 320 px; px text ignores iOS Dynamic Type.
Fix: Set the floor at 320 px, use rem, never disable zoom, define a narrow-layout rule, add a 320 px / 200 % Playwright check, verify 1.4.12.

Verdict: fits at 100 % text on a 360 px viewport with almost no headroom, and breaks under real-world conditions the spec explicitly promises to support. Measured from font metrics and headless renders: the grid overflows at 110 % OS text or below about 352 px; the strip overflows at about 130 % and immediately with the spec's own 999 999 format; 640 px is a device height, but the usable browser viewport is about 510–560 px. The only large-text rule ("labels drop first") does not free the width that is short. Counts: 1 critical, 6 high, 8 medium, 6 low.

critical

1. The 36-column grid has no headroom and no reflow rule for 200 % text

DESIGN → Typography, Do/Don't; EXPERIENCE → Accessibility Floor
At 360 px, 36 columns fit with 29 px slack at 100 %; at 110 % they need 333.6 px of 332 (measured). At 320 px they fail at 100 %. At 200 % about 20 columns fit, so every box panel, 24-cell meter and aligned table overflows. Android applies OS font size as text scale or page zoom; either way fewer than 36 columns.
Fix: Make column count an output: cols = floor(contentWidth / (0.6 × bodySize)), 36 as target, 28 as box-drawing floor; add a reflow table (≥ 36 / 28–35 / < 28); allow 8 px margins below 360 px; the grid is never a reason to clip text.
high

2. The strip overflows with the spec's own number format

DESIGN → Resource strip, Typography; EXPERIENCE → Resource strip
Five 999 999 values need 316 px plus gaps at 100 %; the binding width is the value, not the label; mockups already print 12.4K; the strip renders 69 px tall against a 56 px token.
Fix: ≤ 5-char values (suffix at 10 000), ≤ 5-char deltas (drop /s), five equal cells, 3 cells + +2 ▸ above 125 %, min-content height.
high

3. The vertical budget assumes a 640 px viewport

EXPERIENCE → Foundation, form_factor; DESIGN → Layout & Spacing; mockups
NEST needs about 580–593 px; a 360×640 Android in Chrome gives about 512, iPhone SE Safari about 548. At 512 the tab bar is pushed off-screen. FRONT → MAP is already 764 px in a 720 px frame.
Fix: Restate the minimum as a 360×512 viewport; flexible scene height with clamp(); 1 event line under 560 px; 64 px pinned primary; 100dvh.
high

4. Scanlines and glow drop dim text below AA; CRT default unspecified

DESIGN → Elevation & Depth, vector.scanlines, vector.glow; EXPERIENCE → Settings; mockups
On scanline rows dim/bg falls to 3.6–4.0:1; fractional DPRs produce moiré; mockups give dim text an accent halo brighter than the glyph (3.2–3.75:1).
Fix: Tri-state CRT: SCENE (default) / FULL / OFF; scanlines over the vector scene only; glow ≤ 2 px on accent text ≥ 14 px; device-pixel scanlines; auto-OFF on prefers-contrast or > 130 % text; measure contrast with effects on.
high

5. The platform monospace stack can't guarantee the grid glyphs

DESIGN → Typography, Meter, Shapes, Colors
ui-monospace resolves only in Safari; Android gets Droid Sans Mono; Liberation Mono lacks ✓/✗ and the fallback is 47 % wider per glyph, so any row with a check mark falls off the grid.
Fix: Ship one 15–25 KB subset mono web font; or restrict grid rows to ASCII plus box/block glyphs and render ✓ ✗ ▲ ▼ as 1ch inline SVG with a device width test.
high

6. The bottom-40 % reach rule can't hold on WRKR or BUILD

EXPERIENCE → Accessibility Floor; DESIGN → spacing.thumb-zone
On a 512 px viewport the bottom 40 % is 205 px; tab bar and pinned primary leave about 64 px, one row. Written as an E1 done-when, it becomes a failing acceptance test.
Fix: Reword to cover the primary action, tab bar and active sheet actions; pad scroll content so any row can reach the zone; bottom-anchor WRKR with GATHER nearest the thumb.
high

7. Map tiles: 40 px with 1 px gaps mis-tap, and the sheet hides the map

DESIGN → Map tile; EXPERIENCE → Map tile, Accessibility Floor
Estimated miss rate 6–20 % on a 6.4 mm target; at 320 px tiles shrink to 35 px unspecified; a 200–250 px sheet covers rows 5–8 so a mis-selection is invisible.
Fix: Non-modal ≤ 160 px peek sheet that retargets on tap; auto-scroll the selected tile; 2 px gaps; stroke ≥ 45 %; pan rather than shrink below 352 px; hide the pinned FRONT primary while the sheet is open.
medium

8. 15 px values can't share a grid row with 14 px body

DESIGN → Typography, Role row, Stepper, Card
A 15 px character is 9.0 px against an 8.4 px cell; columns drift across rows.
Fix: Render grid-row values at 14 px bold, or in fixed slots; keep 15 px off-grid.
medium

9. 11 px dim labels and 12 px dim meta are below comfortable reading size

DESIGN → Typography, Resource strip, Event line, Card cost line
An 11 px cap subtends about 7.5′ at arm's length against a 12′ critical print size; the 12 px cost line carries the shortfall the player must act on.
Fix: label to 12 px; no dim below 12 px, no sentence case below 13 px; cost line 13 px with the shortfall in warn.
medium

Workers render as 9 × 3 px pills; eggs and larvae at 5–6 px, yet telling them apart is the Phase 1 loop.

Code feasibility review

review-code-feasibility.md

Verdict: feasible with changes, checked against main @ 24da5b1. Nothing is impossible in the stack and nothing breaks the repo rules if stories are written as suggested. The spec is more aligned with the GDD than with the code; most conflicts are pre-GDD baseline behaviour still in code. Three decisions are needed before stories are cut: CONTINUE semantics, the check-in resume path, and the decision-event lifecycle. The "Implementation notes from Discovery" table is accurate but understates the work. Status of 37 spec items: 2 already implemented, 15 small change, 12 significant change, 8 conflicts. Findings: 1 critical, 5 high, 9 medium, 8 low.

critical

C-1. CONTINUE semantics are undefined, and Colony has two exits

EXPERIENCE → Phase change; src/phases/PhaseStateMachine.ts:26-38, transitions.ts:31,71
The FSM fires and commits in one step. Guards can stop holding after being met (starvation, unassigned guard, spent VC); the spec doesn't say whether CONTINUE is latched or live or what happens if the guard fails before the tap. Colony has two outgoing transitions, so "the" CONTINUE target is ambiguous.
Fix: Spec: CONTINUE is offered while the guard holds, re-evaluated every tick and re-checked on tap; on failure the button reverts to the meter showing the failing row. Stories: remove COLONY_TO_EXPANSION and migrate its E2E seeds; split the FSM into pure evaluate() and commit() called only from CONTINUE; seeds click a data-action="continue" selector.
