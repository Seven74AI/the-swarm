Vector glyphs in the terminal UI

One line-icon style: a 24×24 grid with a 1.6 px stroke and round caps. The glyphs take the current phase's phosphor colour, so they repaint with each phase like the text does. 20 glyphs in total (14 resources and 6 buildings), which covers the GDD's "~20 resources + 6 buildings" icon set. They're all hand-drawable SVG, a few KB in total.
